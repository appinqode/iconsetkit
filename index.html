Create App Icon Assets

Iconset Kit is a simple and efficient utility for Apple developers and designers who need to create App Icon asset catalogs for Xcode. Instead of manually resizing images and preparing dozens of icon variations, Iconset Kit generates all required app icon sizes from a single source image and exports a ready-to-use .appiconset folder. Simply select an image, preview the generated icons, and export the asset catalog directly into your project. Perfect for independent developers, students, hobbyists, and professional teams building apps for Apple's platforms.
